### Note on OQ's publication record
Searches of arXiv, IEEE and Google Scholar return **no significant peer-reviewed publications under an OQ Technology affiliation**. Adjacent academic work on NB-IoT-over-LEO (synchronisation, resource allocation, NTN surveys) comes from universities and other vendors. OQ's public technical output is: **granted and pending patents, ESA ARTES project deliverables (MACSAT, CREST-5G, BEAMSAT-5G), 3GPP participation, GSMA membership, and press-released in-orbit demonstrations.** If you want to cite "their" technical work, cite the patents and the ESA projects by name.
