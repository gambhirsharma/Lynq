# OQ ONE

**The Smartest 5G NB-IoT Terminal with Dual-Mode Functionality – Satellite & Cellular**

*By OQ Technology*

## Overview

Designed by **OQ Technology**, the world's first global satellite 5G IoT operator, OQ ONE is a flexible, rugged, and programmable IoT terminal offering pre-paid data packages. This terminal is specifically designed for remote monitoring and controlling of both fixed and mobile assets across various industries such as transportation, oil and gas, utilities, maritime, agriculture, and more. Capable of connecting up to 1000 sensors, OQ ONE provides extensive connectivity options. Moreover, it is powered by a long-lasting battery that keeps it operational for several years. The simplicity of its use underscores the smart design of OQ ONE, marking it one of the most innovative products in the satellite technology spectrum.

## Key Highlights

### Tracking Mode

Our tracker determines its position based on GNSS satellite signals and sends it autonomously over OQTech's Satellite Network.

### Geo-Fencing

Equipped with a built-in GPS/GNSS module offering an impressive accuracy of approximately 5 meters, OQ ONE delivers cutting-edge geo-fencing capabilities. This empowers customers to effortlessly configure essential mechanisms, such as transmission control and restriction, auto shutoff, and more. By leveraging these advanced features, OQ ONE ensures the utmost level of protection against theft and unauthorized usage, setting the industry standard for security.

### Monitoring Mode

The terminal offers the flexibility to connect either a passive or a smart sensor. In this mode, the gathered data seamlessly integrates with the GPS coordinates, forming a comprehensive transmission package. By combining sensor data with precise location information, the system provides enhanced monitoring capabilities, enabling users to access a wealth of contextual insights.

### IoT Platform

A comprehensive plug-and-play management platform empowers users with complete control over their network and seamless access to their data. With an intuitive interface, users can effortlessly view and analyze their data, while also having the ability to easily manage and configure their network settings. The platform ensures a user-friendly experience, enabling efficient data management and network control.
